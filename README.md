# Pizza Underground

Pizza has been banned. Nobody knows why. You and your friends decide to become the town's biggest illegal pizza business.

A low-poly co-op comedy for 1-4 players, in the browser.

- BUY INGREDIENTS → MAKE PIZZA → TAKE ORDERS → DELIVER → MAKE MONEY → EXPAND → HIDE FROM POLICE → UPGRADE → TAKE BIGGER RISKS.
- POLICE INSPECTIONS: the police collect tips (deliveries, reports, smoke, fires) and roll the dice every second. When it hits: INSPECTION IN 45... 30... 15... 5, 4, 3. Hide the pizzas in the "shoe" fridge, put out the ovens, disguise the stock fridge, flip the specials board, close the secret hatch, move the cash into the safe, drive the delivery car away. Then two officers walk in and check everything while you act normal. Pass, get fined, lose half your ingredients - or get SHUT DOWN.
- Everything weighs something. Vehicles carry KG, not "slots": the oven is 70 KG, a crate of flour 10, cheese 5. Overload it and you get TOO HEAVY - VEHICLE CAPACITY: 100 KG. A loaded vehicle is slower, and so are you when you carry a big crate.
- Your first ride is the free RUSTY SCOOTER (Dez "found" it): 100 KG, rust, duct tape, it coughs smoke and wobbles. Then HONEST HANK'S MOTORS (north of the gas station: a glass showroom with cars on turntables and spec cards, a service garage, a lot of big trucks, a pick-up bay): Delivery Moped ($2,500, 180 KG), Small Delivery Car ($7,500, 350 KG), Delivery Van ($20,000, 750 KG), Cargo Van ($50,000, 1,500 KG), Mafia Transport Truck ($120,000, 3,500 KG), the Massive Pizza Transporter ($300,000, a trailer shaped like a pizza box) - each its own model, with doors and trunks that open and the cargo visibly packed inside. Special orders too: the getaway car, the Family Sedan, the ice cream truck, the armored truck.
- The Crumb Mall: CRUMB GROCERY (produce stands, carts, fridges, freezers, a loading dock - crates of ingredients off real shelves), EQUIP-O-RAMA (an industrial warehouse: equipment comes in big crates you load, drive home and install), CASA CRUMB FURNITURE (display rooms: a sofa, a jukebox, an arcade machine, a boss desk... place them in the hideout, customers tip more) and the general store.
- Load boxes and crates into a vehicle (E at the back), deliver straight out of the truck bed, unload a car full of crates into the hideout in one go.
- A town that lives: downtown towers, rich Crumb Heights (pools, topiaries), poor Lower Crumbville (a car on blocks, a couch on the lawn), the industrial east, a construction site with a working crane, a utility crew up a bucket truck, a man in a manhole, street vendors, dog walkers, people with shopping bags, a garbage truck that stops to chomp.
- Always something happening: rush hours, supplier vans, tow trucks, grocery shortages, storms (cars slide, tips +50%), police informants, a task list on screen.
- It starts in the car: a crappy delivery car, Dez riding shotgun, a pizza for City Hall, two minutes late. Then a limo. Then the hospital, where the doctor has good news and bad news, and Dez is stuck in a wall.
- Crumbville: a town square with a statue of the mayor, City Hall, the hospital, the police station, a gas station, shops, dead pizzerias, back alleys, houses, a cardboard factory, a junkyard, a park and a forest. Signs everywhere: PIZZA IS ILLEGAL. REPORT SUSPICIOUS CHEESE ACTIVITY.
- A suspicious man in a trench coat pays you $15,000 for one pizza. Find the abandoned shoe repair shop, clean it, hit the fuse box until it works, buy an oven from Oleg ("for bread"), buy ingredients from underground suppliers (half of them are toppings: Tomato Tony, Big Cheese, Sal Ami, Pineapple Pete...) and watch out for Legit Larry.
- Cook for real: dough from the tub, flatten it (hold E), sauce/cheese/toppings with 1-7, bake it, take it out when it's golden, box it. Burn it, over-cheese it until it explodes, let the grease build up until the oven catches fire, and watch the fire spread across the kitchen while another order waits.
- Orders arrive on your phone (TAB). Some customers are undercover cops. Their messages are not subtle.
- The police patrol the sidewalks and drive the streets. Get seen with pizza and their suspicion meter fills. Run, break line of sight, hide in a dumpster, or hold X to act natural. Inspectors raid the hideout - hide the evidence in the "DEFINITELY SHOES" fridge.
- Random events: THE INSPECTOR IS COMING, YOUR OVEN IS ON FIRE, A CUSTOMER ORDERED 100 PIZZAS, THE CHEESE SUPPLIER IS MISSING, A POLICE CAR IS OUTSIDE, SOME IDIOT POSTED YOUR LOCATION ONLINE, and new pizza laws nobody understands.
- Grow the hideout from a tiny kitchen to a secret basement, an underground factory and a pizza empire. Hire a cook and a driver, buy cameras, sprinklers, a hidden entrance, disguises and better vehicles (an ice cream truck is the perfect cover).
- The pizza mafia: customers sometimes put it "on the tab", and once the town knows you, businesses run tabs too. Knock on their door (they pay, they make excuses, or they beg for a deadline), send Knuckles the debt collector, or - once the deadline has passed - confiscate their lawn flamingo / TV / shop sign until they pay. It sits in the hidden storage room under the yard (with the money bags and the secret safe), and goes home when they do. Nobody gets hurt. "Uh... I thought you guys forgot." "We did."
- The Underground Market (a secret; it's not on the map): Pages & Pages Used Books on Pepper Road sells books. Only books. The owner, Vito, is very nervous and very hungry. Bring him a pizza: he checks nobody is watching, walks to the back shelf, pulls a red book, and the whole bookcase swings away to stairs going down. Below is a dim cellar market: crates, jars of pickled ???, cash, a cork board of secret documents, locked glass cases, the DOUGH-TRON 3000, a cheese wheel that bites, a VIP room behind a bead curtain, a steel door nobody opens, the Broker, the regulars, and twelve tables of very fictional gear.
- Gear (B: your gear, click or C: use it; it goes away while you carry things): the Big Foam Bat (BONK: a cop forgets why he was chasing you), the Comically Huge Mallet (cop pancake), the Heavy Toolbox (fixes wrecked ovens), Cardboard Knuckles (debtors pay up), the Party Smoke Machine (inspection fog, -3 evidence), the Lock-Breaking Kit (a rubber chicken that skips a debt's deadline), the Instant Disguise Kit (glasses, nose, mustache: cops ignore you for 45s), the Air Horn (cops freeze), the Very Bright Flashlight (finds clues, blinds chasers), the Decoy Pizza Box (throw it, the cops chase the box), Fake Documents (a cop salutes and leaves; -4 evidence) and the Suspicious Briefcase ($2,000 and a muffin end a chase). Each one has its own model and goofy equip, idle, walk, use, throw and put-away animations, in first and third person.
- Rival pizza gangs: The Italian Guys (a "criminal empire" in a falling-apart garage), The Delivery Boys (fast vans, terrible pizza) and The Frozen Pizza Gang (supermarket frozen pizza, $40 a box). Each has a boss, a building and an office. They grow if you ignore them (garage -> GARAGE EMPIRE), race you to customers, steal orders, raid your hideout, set up a pizza stand two blocks away, steal your cheese, offer deals and partnerships (allies help you), invite you to meetings ("We have a problem. ... You sell pizza. ... We sell pizza." SPLAT. "...Frank.") and give you missions: a delivery race, beat the depot record, the missing shipment, take over MAMMA MIA'S, the mayor's huge order, hide their crates from the inspectors, find a secret kitchen.
- Sabotage them by hand: sneak in (front or back door) past guards and cameras, empty the register, sabotage the oven, knock over a shelf, salt the sauce / steal the van keys / unplug the freezer, carry out their crates, steal their trophy and put it on display. Get caught and they drag you to the back room to argue (you wake up outside your hideout, hurt, and they have visited your kitchen). Nothing is lost for good.
- Working security cameras, theirs and yours (laptop: Security Cameras). Cameras see people: MOTION DETECTED, which camera, where. They record: the monitor (K) has live feeds and grainy black-and-white recordings with timestamps you can rewind, scrub and save as evidence. Hand evidence in at the police station: the police may fine the rival, shut part of them down, watch their place - or decide it's a raccoon. Block a camera with a pizza box, smash it with a bat, cut its wire with the toolbox or throw something at it; they notice dead cameras eventually.
- A hotbar and an inventory. The hotbar (bottom of the screen, keys 1-9 and 0) holds your gear, smoke bombs and trash bags; new things drop into the first free slot. Press a number to pull it out, again to put it away. I opens the inventory: tabs for Gear, Supplies, Outfits and Vehicles (Q / E to switch), a grid of everything you own, a turntable of you holding or wearing the selected thing (drag to rotate), and a card with its stars, stats and description. Point at something and press a number to put it on that hotbar slot. Every icon is the item's own 3D model, rendered by the game.
- The Comically Large Trash Bag (general store): bag a debtor at their door (R), carry the wriggling bag to a car, put it in the trunk (it takes 4 space; it doesn't fit on a scooter), drive to the hideout and take them down to the hidden storage room's Time-Out Chair. There they get a black hood over the head and handcuffs behind the chair. At first they refuse and threaten to call the cops; bonk them with the foam bat or the mallet and their resolve drops (left alone in the dark it drops slowly too) until they agree to pay. Then take the money - or don't, and keep them in the chair for good. You can always let them go home, still owing you. Drop the bag, get busted or leave them too long and they wriggle out and run home. Cops treat the bag as evidence.
- Driving in first person: a low-poly cockpit with a dashboard, a speedometer, a steering wheel that turns with your hands on it, windshield pillars, a rear-view mirror and side mirrors.
- Mafia Reputation: from "Who?" to "Legends of the Underground". Each tier unlocks situations: business tabs, nervous customers who tip more, Knuckles, the Suspicious Suit, VIP orders, the Family Sedan, free oven repairs from Oleg, the Calzone Cartel (settle it with rock-paper-scissors), the Sit-Down with City Hall, and the Pizza Summit.
- Every building can be walked into, each furnished for what it is: houses with kitchens and sofas, shops with aisles, the police station with desks and a holding cell, City Hall with its red carpet and a portrait of the very hungry mayor, abandoned pizzerias with chairs on the tables. The hideout kitchen has a brick dome oven with a real fire, a steel prep table with a topping rail, a dough mixer, a heated pass, a sink, and shelves of supplies.
- Find the eight clues and work out why pizza was banned. It's worse than you think. And stupider.
- Co-op for up to four players over WebRTC (PeerJS): host a room, share the five-letter code. One kitchen, one purse, one heat meter.
- The dialogue swears (it's for teenagers). Tick "Bleep the swearing" on the title screen or in the pause menu to turn it into #$%&!.

Everything - the town, the characters, the toppings, the cars, the signs and every sound - is generated in code. No asset files.

Owner tools: the admin panel opens with the key sequence J, L, O, 3 (host or solo player only).

Controls: G smoke bomb, V first/third person, WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, mouse look, wheel zoom, E interact (hold for some things), R second action, Q throw away, X act natural, 1-7 toppings, F car, H honk, TAB phone, M map, T chat, ESC pause, LMB spray an extinguisher or use your gear, C use gear, 1-0 hotbar, I inventory, K security cameras.

Run it: serve the folder with any static server (`python -m http.server`) and open it. After changing any .js file run `node tools/stamp.mjs` so browsers fetch the new code.

Tests: `/?script=all`, `/?script=input` (results drawn on screen), `/_nettest.html` (two players in one page), `/?skip=11&shot=hideout` (jump into the story).
